### Key Concepts

- **Relevance Realization**: How cognitive systems determine what matters from infinite possibilities
- **4E Cognition**: Embodied, Embedded, Enacted, Extended cognition framework
- **Four Ways of Knowing**: Propositional, Procedural, Perspectival, Participatory
- **Wisdom Cultivation**: Systematic optimization of relevance realization
- **Sophrosyne**: Balanced judgment and optimal self-regulation
- **Opponent Processing**: Balancing cognitive tradeoffs (exploration/exploitation, speed/accuracy)
- **Meta-Cognition**: Recursive self-monitoring and awareness of thinking processes
